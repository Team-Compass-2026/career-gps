## salary_data

Salary ranges are illustrative estimates and vary by region, company, and experience. Do not present as guarantees.

Example (U.S., 2024-ish, clearly labeled as estimates): Data Analyst entry ~USD 50–70k, mid ~70–95k. Data Scientist entry ~USD 80–110k. Software Engineer entry ~USD 70–120k. Use local sources before quoting.
