# Career GPS

**Team:** Team Compass🧭 · **Track:** T2 — Education Equity · **Hackathon:** DEEP

## Problem

Young people today have abundant access to career information through social media, online courses, influencers and various platforms. However, this abundance often creates confusion rather than clear and reliable information. Many young people either remain stagnant because they don't know which advice to follow without a clear career strategy. As a result, young people waste a lot of time and money developing skills that turn out to be irrelevant while lacking practical experience, guidance and professional connections.

**Key statistics:**
- A massive 69% of students in the Gen Z Report say that thinking about their future careers causes them significant stress. Meanwhile, 49% of those in the FE Choices Survey felt pressure from their parents to make good career decisions. — Youth Survey, 2025
- 44% of surveyed Myanmar youth could not imagine what their future jobs and skills would look like. — JICA, Myanmar Report, 2016
- Three in four young people aged 18–24 are out of school or training, while young people with higher education are still struggling to find jobs. — UNDP Myanmar Youth survey, 2025
- ~80% of Myanmar employers reported a mismatch between skills taught and skills demanded by industry. — MYANMAR'S FUTURE JOBS: EMBRACING MODERNITY MAIN REPORT, 2018

## Root Causes

- Advice is generic, often designed for a broad audience rather than an individual's interests, abilities, financial situation, or goals
- Conflicting advice makes us more doubtful ("learn PowerBI, be a data analyst" vs "AI is the future, do database engineering" vs "do arts or study medicine which AI cannot replace")
- Too many possible pathways don't necessarily produce better decisions ("The patient is dead due to the multiple treatments from different doctors")
- Students lack a clear career goal, strong self-awareness, uncertainty about career outcomes, fear of making the wrong choice, structured decision-making and personalized roadmap

## Current Challenges

There is tons of information out there, which helps us with understanding, preparing and getting ready for the career we would like to pursue. However, most of us do not progress overtime. We know what we need to do but we barely put effort and consistency due to several reasons. This abundance often creates confusion rather than clear and reliable information. Many young people either remain stagnant because they don't know which advice to follow without a clear career strategy. As a result, young people waste a lot of time and money developing skills that turn out to be irrelevant while lacking practical experience, guidance and professional connections.

## Solution

**Career GPS** is a Personalised Career Companion that stays with you until you reach the milestone. Our slogan: *We stay with you until you do it.*

Our solution transforms career goals into clear and actionable pathways. Based on each user's goals, current skills and experience, the platform recommends next steps to prioritize such as relevant courses, projects, internships and career opportunities along with an achievable timeline.

Users receive ongoing support, accountability and access to experienced mentors from their desired fields. By connecting young people with the right information, people, and opportunities at the right time, we aim to help them break free from career confusion to achieve sustainable career growth. We provide human reinforcement alongside technology.

## How It Is Different From Other Solutions

**Competitor analysis:**
- Student → meets mentor → talks for 1 hour → make progress → maybe meets again next month (or) Student → meets mentor → talks for 1 hour → disappear → never meet again

**Our platform converts all the efforts, passions and interests into the actual ACTIONS with progress tracking, internship opportunities, and so on.**

- Mentor Tiers: students choose the level of support they need and we can put flexibility in pricing as well
  - Community mentor: Group sessions, Volunteer/reputation-based
  - Professional mentor: Individual session
  - Industry mentor: Specialised expertise
  - Career advisor: High-touch support

- Digital Entrepreneurship and technology integration in our business model: We use technology to create a continuous career-support system that remembers a student's goals, tracks their progress, coordinates mentors and opportunities, detects when they are falling behind and intervenes before they give up. We are building a technology-enabled career development service that coordinates personalized guidance, human mentorship, accountability, and real-world opportunities.

## User Journey

**Step 1:** Finding career path based on their personality, interests and skills
**Step 2:** Connecting with a Mentor
**Step 3:** Making regular progress based on the Mentor's instruction and suggestion
**Step 4:** Linking with practical world, Internship
**Step 5:** Preparing for a job interview/ Connection
**Step 6:** Landing a job

**Career Support Journey:**
Discover → Prepare → Experience → Launch → Grow

## Impacts

- Reduce career confusion stress for young people
- Decrease time and money wasted on irrelevant skills
- Increase practical experience through internship opportunities
- Improve job placement rates for graduates
- Bridge the skills gap between education and industry demands

## Business Model

- Mentor Tiers with flexible pricing (Community/Professional/Industry/Career advisor)
- Students choose the level of support they need
- Platform provides roadmap, tasks, progress tracking, reminders, accountability and reporting
- Mentors contribute experience, feedback, perspective, professional advice, industry context and networking
- Students contribute execution and progress tracking

## Market Entry Strategies

- Launch with curated careers (10-20) and high-quality small dataset (not web crawl)
- Focus on Education Equity track: guidance without paid counselors or elite networks
- Start with profile → career recommendations → skill gap → roadmap flow
- Integrate with existing education ecosystems

## Core User Flow (MVP)

1. Landing → sign up (Better Auth) → create profile
2. Career assessment → 3–5 recommendations with explanations
3. Select target career → skill-gap table
4. Personalized roadmap (phased, time-aware)
5. AI Career Assistant (structured data) for follow-up questions / roadmap tweaks
6. Progress: mark skills / milestones complete

**Demo persona: Alex** (CS student, basic Python/HTML/Excel, 10h/week, Data Analyst goal).

## MVP (must)

- User profile (education, skills, interests, goals, available hours, preferences)
- Career recommendations (scored guidance indicator)
- Skill gap analysis
- Personalized roadmap (structured data)
- Progress tracking (basic)

## Target Users

University / high-school / college students, recent graduates, career switchers, self-learners lacking counselors or networks.

## Ethics & Privacy

Recommendations are guidance, not destiny. Cite when possible; no discriminatory routing; collect minimum data; PII not in public data corpus; users can control/delete profile data (design for MVP+).
